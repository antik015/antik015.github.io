I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Statistics](https://www.stat.purdue.edu/) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/).

My research focuses on **Bayesian statistics, probabilistic graphical models, computational statistics, and Monte Carlo methods**, with an emphasis on scalable methodology for complex, high-dimensional problems. Applications include preference and ranking data, ecology, actuarial science, and other scientific settings.

Before joining Purdue, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in Statistical Science at Duke University, working with [David Dunson](https://stat.duke.edu/people/david-b-dunson). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Texas A&M University under the supervision of [Bani K. Mallick](https://artsci.tamu.edu/statistics/contact/profiles/bani-mallick.html) and [Anirban Bhattacharya](https://artsci.tamu.edu/statistics/contact/profiles/anirban-bhattacharya.html).

I currently serve as an **Associate Editor of Sankhyā A**. My research is partially funded by the **U.S. National Science Foundation** and the **Society of Actuaries**.
