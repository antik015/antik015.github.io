## Previous teaching

- Spring 2021, STA 101, at [Duke University](https://www.duke.edu/)
- Summer II 2016, STAT 302, at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/)
- STAT 633 at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/): gave six lectures for the Advanced Bayesian Methods course intended for Ph.D. students. Topics covered included posterior consistency and posterior contraction in high-dimensional and nonparametric Bayesian methods. I prepared a semi-formal set of [notes](https://antik015.github.io/files/bayes_rates.pdf) based on the topics covered. These notes are mainly based on *Fundamentals of Bayesian Nonparametrics* by Ghosal and van der Vaart and primarily cover parts of Chapters 7, 8, and 9.
